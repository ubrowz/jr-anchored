
Description: Compare the AUCs of TWO continuous tests measured on the SAME
             subjects against a common binary reference standard — the
             correlated-ROC case. Reports each test's AUC with a confidence
             interval, the difference in AUCs with a confidence interval, the
             correlation between the two AUCs, and a DeLong test of
             H0: AUC_A = AUC_B that accounts for the two tests being measured
             on the same people. Saves a two-panel PNG (overlaid ROC curves;
             difference with CI) to ~/Downloads/.
             AUCs, their covariance and the paired test use the placement-value
             method of DeLong, DeLong & Clarke-Pearson (1988) — the method
             written for exactly this correlated comparison (Hanley & McNeil
             1983). Needs ggplot2 (plot only; statistics are base R).

Usage: jrc_clinical_dx_compare <data.csv> [--tests colA,colB] [--conf C]
                               [--direction {higher|lower}] [--positive LABEL]
                               [--ci-method {delong|logit}]

Arguments:
       data.csv      CSV with columns: id, reference, and two score columns.
                       id         subject identifier (unique)
                       reference  reference-standard condition
                       <two score columns>  the two continuous tests
                     Labels accepted for reference (case-insensitive): 1/0,
                     pos/neg, positive/negative, yes/no, true/false, +/-.
       --tests       the two score column names, comma-separated
                     (e.g. --tests marker_a,marker_b). If omitted and the file
                     has exactly two columns besides id and reference, those
                     two are used.
       --conf        two-sided confidence level in (0, 1); default 0.95.
       --direction   higher  higher score => more likely positive (default)
                     lower   lower score  => more likely positive
                     Applied to BOTH tests. If the two tests read in opposite
                     directions, flip one test's sign in the CSV first.
       --positive    label to treat as positive in the reference column.
       --ci-method   delong AUC +/- z*SE on the raw scale (default)
                     logit  per-test AUC CI on the logit scale, back-
                            transformed. The difference CI is always raw-scale.
       -h| --help    Show this help message

Notes: Both tests must be measured on every subject (paired data) — that is
       what the DeLong covariance term corrects for; running two separate
       jrc_clinical_dx_roc analyses and comparing their CIs by eye is NOT a
       valid test, because it ignores the correlation. A cutoff or operating
       point is not reported here; use jrc_clinical_dx_roc for that on a single
       test.

Example:
       jrc_clinical_dx_compare paired.csv
       jrc_clinical_dx_compare paired.csv --tests marker_a,marker_b --conf 0.90
       jrc_clinical_dx_compare paired.csv --direction lower
