
Description: ANCOVA — compare a continuous outcome across groups while
             ADJUSTING for one or more baseline covariates. Reports the Type III
             joint tests (is the group effect significant after adjustment?),
             the adjusted least-squares means per group with confidence
             intervals, Tukey-adjusted pairwise group contrasts, and each
             covariate's effect. Adjusted means and Type III tests use the
             validated emmeans package; the model is base R lm.

Usage: jrc_clinical_ancova <data.csv> --covariates c1,c2,... [--conf C]

Arguments:
       data.csv      CSV with columns: id, group, value, and one column per
                     covariate named in --covariates.
                       id     subject identifier (unique)
                       group  grouping factor of interest (2+ levels)
                       value  the continuous outcome (numeric)
                     A covariate may be numeric or categorical (factor).
       --covariates  REQUIRED. Comma-separated covariate column names to adjust
                     for, e.g. --covariates baseline,age.
       --conf        two-sided confidence level in (0, 1); default 0.95
       -h| --help    Show this help message

Notes: ANCOVA is the standard "outcome adjusted for baseline" analysis (ICH E9).
       The 'group' row of the Type III joint tests is the covariate-adjusted
       test of whether the group means differ. Needs the pinned emmeans package;
       a benign "built under R 4.6.1" warning may appear (TROUBLESHOOTING #15).

Example:
       jrc_clinical_ancova trial.csv --covariates baseline
       jrc_clinical_ancova trial.csv --covariates baseline,age --conf 0.90
