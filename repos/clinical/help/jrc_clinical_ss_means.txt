
Description: Computes the two-arm parallel-group sample size for a continuous
             (measured) primary endpoint, for a superiority, non-inferiority,
             or equivalence (TOST) design. Reports evaluable n per arm and in
             total, optionally inflated for dropout, with an optional
             sensitivity table over the assumed SD.
             Method: normal-approximation two-sample formulas
             (Chow, Shao & Wang 2008, Chapter 3). Base R only.

Usage: jrc_clinical_ss_means --framework <fw> --power P --alpha A --sides {1|2}
                             --sd SD [--delta D] [--margin M] [--ratio R]
                             [--dropout d] [--sensitivity]

Arguments:
       --framework   superiority | non_inferiority | equivalence
       --power       target power, e.g. 0.80 (pivotal studies often 0.90)
       --alpha       significance level (superiority 0.05 two-sided;
                     non-inferiority 0.025 one-sided; equivalence TOST 0.05)
       --sides       1 or 2 (must match the framework's convention)
       --sd          expected common SD of the outcome
       --delta       superiority: clinically meaningful difference (required);
                     NI/equivalence: optional assumed true difference (default 0)
       --margin      non-inferiority / equivalence margin M (required there)
       --ratio       allocation treatment:control, default 1
       --dropout     expected dropout fraction; adds the enrolled N line
       --sensitivity print an n-vs-SD scenario table (SD x 0.8 ... 1.2)
       -h| --help    Show this help message

Example:
       jrc_clinical_ss_means --framework superiority --power 0.80 --alpha 0.05 --sides 2 --sd 10 --delta 5
       jrc_clinical_ss_means --framework non_inferiority --power 0.90 --alpha 0.025 --sides 1 --sd 8 --margin 4 --dropout 0.15
